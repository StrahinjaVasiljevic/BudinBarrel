FAMILY TRADITION

Ista paralela kao Bordo i Toskana.

The same parallel as Bordeaux and Tuscany.

Naša destilerija nalazi se u Despotovom gradu, na vratima Šumadije, u Smederevu — na 45. paraleli, istoj liniji koja povezuje Bordo i Toskanu. Okružena starim vinogradima i voćnjacima domaće šljive i kajsije, ova oblast je oduvek bila idealna za voćarstvo i vinogradarstvo.

Our distillery sits in the Despot's Town, at the gateway to Šumadija, in Smederevo — on the 45th parallel, the same line that connects Bordeaux and Tuscany. Surrounded by old vineyards and orchards of native plum and apricot, this region has always been suited to fruit and vine.

“Taste it — it brings back the age of the Nemanjić.”

Single Barrel proizvodnja.

Barrels that remember the craft.

Svako bure nosi ručno rezbareni otisak, poput lika Svetog Georgija - zaštitnika kuće Vasiljević. Burad su od hrasta kitnjaka (Quercus petraea), koji rakiji daje boju ćilibara i blagu notu vanile i suvog voća.

Every barrel carries a hand-carved figure of St. George — the Vasiljević house's patron and a mark that follows each batch from the moment the brandy enters the wood. The barrels are made of sessile oak (Quercus petraea), which gives the brandy its amber colour and a gentle note of vanilla and dried fruit.

Ručna proizvodnja od početka do kraja.

Handmade from start to finish.

Sve počinje u našim voćnjacima šljive i kajsije i u vinogradima loze, gde plod bere ruka, ne mašina. Odatle put vodi u destileriju, pa u podrum, gde rakija strpljivo odležava u hrastovim buretima dok ne dobije boju i miris kojim stojimo iza svake flaše.

It all starts in our plum and apricot orchards and in the vineyards, where the fruit is picked by hand, not machine. From there it goes to the distillery, then down to the cellar, where the brandy patiently ages in oak barrels until it earns the colour and aroma we put our name behind.
