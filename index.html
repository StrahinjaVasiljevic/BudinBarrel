Porodična tradicija
Family Tradition

Kao Bordo, kao Toskana

Like Bordeaux. Like Tuscany.

Naša destilerija nalazi se u Despotovom gradu, na vratima Šumadije, u Smederevu i 45. severnoj paraleli. Na istoj širini nalaze se Bordo i Toskana, najpogodniji krajevi u Evropi za voćarstvo i vinogradarstvo. Ova oblast neguje tradiciju proizvodnje pića još od Srednjeg veka.

Our distillery stands in the Despot's Town, at the gateway to Šumadija, in Smederevo — on the 45th parallel, the very line that runs through Bordeaux and Tuscany. Old vineyards and orchards of native plum and apricot surround us; this land has been shaping fruit and vine since the Middle Ages.

„Probajte - vraća u doba Nemanjića!“ "Taste it — and step back into the age of the Nemanjići."

Single Barrel proizvodnja

Barrels that remember the craft

Svaki barrel nosi ručno rezbareni otisak, poput lika Svetog Georgija, zaštitnika kuće Vasiljević. Naša burad su od hrasta kitnjaka (Quercus petraea), koji rakiji daje boju ćilibara i blagu notu vanile i suvog voća.

Every barrel carries a hand-carved figure of St. George, the Vasiljević house's patron and a mark that follows each batch from the moment the brandy enters the wood. The barrels are made of sessile oak (Quercus petraea), which gives the brandy its amber colour and a gentle note of vanilla and dried fruit.

Proces

The Process

Sve počinje berbom u našim voćnjacima i vinogradima. Nastavlja se destilacijom i dugim sazrevanjem u hrastovim buradima. Vreme, priroda i zanatska preciznost oblikuju rakiju iza koje stavljamo ime naše porodice

It all starts with hand-picked fruit from our orchards and vineyards. It continues with distillation and long aging in oak barrels. Time, nature and craftsmanship shape the brandy behind which we proudly stand our name.
